Squirrel Trigger — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-05

This policy explains what data Squirrel Trigger collects and how it is used. It is written to match the current build, verified by a code-level review on 2026-10-05 (export config, Android/iOS manifests, and the only third-party plugin in the project — Google Play Billing). It will be updated if AGIAAAA-505's formal data-safety audit finds anything beyond what is listed here, or if a future release adds analytics, ads, or accounts.

Who controls this data

Squirrel Trigger is made by AgileMind. Contact: support@laslusta.se (shared AgileMind support inbox; update this if a dedicated address is set up).

What we collect

Nothing you type or identify yourself with. Squirrel Trigger has no accounts, no sign-in, no profile, and no email or username field. There is nothing to create or log into.

Game progress stays on your device. High scores, achievements and settings are saved locally on your phone or tablet only. We never receive this data — it is not transmitted anywhere.

No analytics, ads, or crash reporting. The build contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK. We do not track your activity in the app or across other apps and websites.

Subscription purchases go through the app store, not us. If you subscribe, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing (Android) or the App Store (iOS). We receive only the entitlement result — whether your subscription is active — so the app can unlock paid features. We never see your payment method, card number, or billing address. Google's and Apple's own privacy policies cover their part of that transaction:

  • Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Required device permissions. The app may request internet access solely so the app store billing library can verify a subscription. It is not used to send gameplay data, device identifiers, or any personal information to us.

Children

Squirrel Trigger targets a general audience (age rating PEGI 3 / ESRB E or equivalent — cartoon action, no accounts, no data collection). Because we don't collect personal data from any player, we don't knowingly collect personal data from children either.

Data we don't have

Because nothing is sent to us, there is no account data, usage history, device identifier, or advertising ID for us to access, export, or delete. If you have questions about a purchase, contact Google Play or Apple support directly, since they hold that record, not us.

Changes to this policy

If a future update adds analytics, ads, accounts, or server-side features, we will update this policy before that feature ships, and update the store's data-safety / app-privacy declarations to match.
